# Reference audit

Source inspected: `https://variosoft.us/ebay-seller-account-managment/` on 8 October 2026. This is a content/DOM audit; browser screenshots were not captured in this environment. Two Vertix-supplied JPEG brand references were also inspected.

## Verified structure

The reference uses a conversion-led service page: services navigation, quote CTA, hero and meeting CTA, client-logo strip, service overview, service cards, four-step process, add-ons, reasons-to-choose cards, testimonials, FAQs, footer, consent controls, and persistent conversion links. The content is dense but broken into short card-like sections.

## Vertix interpretation

- Preserve the reference's progression from explanation to trust to action, not its copyrighted wording or assets.
- Use Vertix navy, electric blue, white, pale blue surfaces, generous spacing, rounded cards, and restrained motion.
- Use the supplied `Grow Your eBay Store with Expert Support` composition as brand direction, while rebuilding the responsive hero in semantic HTML.
- Do not reuse reference testimonials, client logos, numerical claims, prices, or guarantees.
- Initially publish only approved eBay content for USA, UK, Australia, and Germany.

## Limitations

Exact computed fonts, animation timing, breakpoints, and visual pixel measurements were not verified. These must be checked in browser QA before production release.
